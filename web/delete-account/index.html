Delete your Cash Flow account

For the Cash Flow app on Android and the web

How to delete your account

  1. Open the delete account page with the button below, on any phone or computer. You do not need the app installed.
  2. Sign in with the Google account you use in Cash Flow.
  3. Read what will be removed, type DELETE and choose Delete my account permanently.
Delete my account

In the Android app

While signed in, open Settings and choose Delete account, or tap your name at the bottom of the menu and choose Delete account.

What is deleted

  • Your accounts, entries and change history, in the cloud and on the device you delete from. Accounts you shared are deleted for everyone in them.
  • The Google details Cash Flow holds: your name, email address, photo and sign-in.
  • Your profile, settings and receipt photos on that device.
  • Your place in accounts other people shared with you, invitations sent to you, and the notification registration of your phones.
  • The usage statistics record of the device you delete from: its random ID, country, currency and device type.

What is kept

  • Entries you added to accounts other people own stay in their books, because they are part of those people's records. Your name is removed and they show as made by "Deleted user".
  • A copy on another phone or browser stays on that device until you use Settings, Delete all data on it or remove the app. Its usage statistics record stays until you delete it there, in Settings, Usage statistics.
  • Your Google account itself is not affected. Only its link to Cash Flow is removed.

How long it takes

Deletion happens at once and cannot be undone. Nothing is held back for a waiting period. Technical logs at our hosting provider, Supabase, may mention your account for a short time after that, until they expire on their own. If you sign in again later, you start with a new, empty account.

Never signed in?

Then your books are only on your device: use Settings, Delete all data, or remove the app. The only thing stored online is a usage statistics record with a random ID, your country, currency and device type. Delete it in Settings, Usage statistics before you remove the app.
